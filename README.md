# Exact shell zeros for planar sphere packing

This manuscript is proof of the planar case of Cohn–Elkies Conjecture 8.1. It constructs a sharp sphere-packing certificate whose zeros, and those of its Fourier transform, occur exactly at the distances between distinct points of the hexagonal lattice. Independent review is pending.

- [Proof manuscript](Exact_Shell_Sphere_Packing.pdf) — gives the analytic argument and shows how to reproduce the interval bounds.
- [Lean 4 source](Exact_Shells.lean) — verifies selected lemmas, including how the sign bounds determine the zero set and why 15 cannot be a lattice shell coordinate.
